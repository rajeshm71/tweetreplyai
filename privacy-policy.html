Privacy Policy for TweetReply AI Chrome Extension

Last Updated: October 27, 2025
Effective Date: October 27, 2025

1. Overview

TweetReply AI ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Chrome extension collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our service.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Data We Collect

  • Usage Statistics: Number of replies generated, models used, and general usage patterns
  • Authentication Data: Encrypted tokens for API access (stored locally)
  • User Preferences: Selected AI models, prompt templates, and settings
  • Tweet Content: Only the specific tweet you're replying to (temporarily processed)

2.2 Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Your personal Twitter/X account information
  • Your browsing history outside of Twitter/X
  • Personal identification information
  • Your generated replies (processed locally)
  • Any data from websites other than Twitter/X

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for:

  • Generating AI-powered reply suggestions
  • Maintaining your preferences and settings
  • Providing technical support
  • Improving our service quality
  • Ensuring secure API access

4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 Local Storage

All data is stored locally in your browser using Chrome's secure storage APIs. This includes:

  • Authentication tokens (encrypted)
  • User preferences and settings
  • Usage statistics

4.2 Data Transmission

We only transmit data necessary for AI processing:

  • Tweet content (for context analysis)
  • Selected AI model preferences
  • Authentication tokens (for API access)

4.3 Security Measures

  • All API communications use HTTPS encryption
  • Authentication tokens are encrypted before storage
  • No sensitive data is transmitted to third parties
  • Data is processed securely on our servers

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • AI Model Providers: Third-party AI services for reply generation
  • Authentication: Secure token-based authentication

These services have their own privacy policies, and we ensure they meet our privacy standards.

6. Data Retention

  • Local Data: Retained until you uninstall the extension or clear browser data
  • API Data: Tweet content is processed temporarily and not stored
  • Usage Statistics: Aggregated data may be retained for service improvement

7. Your Rights and Controls

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access: View all data stored locally in your browser
  • Deletion: Clear all data by uninstalling the extension
  • Control: Modify preferences and settings at any time
  • Opt-out: Stop using the extension to halt data collection

8. Chrome Extension Permissions

Our extension requests the following permissions:

  • activeTab: Access current Twitter/X tab only when you click the extension
  • storage: Save your preferences and settings locally
  • scripting: Inject content scripts for text insertion
  • host permissions: Work on twitter.com and x.com domains

9. Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:

  • Updating the "Last Updated" date
  • Posting the new policy on our website
  • Sending notification through the extension (if significant changes)

  • General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
  • California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)
